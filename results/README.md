# Results and Evaluation

This directory contains result visualizations from three main-drone reinforcement learning experiments:

- **SARSA**
- **Q-Learning**
- **Deep Q-Network (DQN)**

These visualizations support the comparison of learning behavior across the available algorithms.

## Evaluation Metrics

The broader research evaluates the algorithms using:
- Average episode reward
- Navigation success rate
- Collision and timeout outcomes

## Reported Results

The research report identifies DQN as the best-performing algorithm in the reported main-drone training results, with an average reward of 140.90 and a success rate of 94.3%.

These figures are reported in the study and have not been independently reproduced through this repository.

Additional visualizations may be added as the remaining experiment outputs are reviewed.
